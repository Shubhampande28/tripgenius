## Track A

### Sri Lanka

**Kandy** — Temps: Wikipedia "Kandy" climate table (highs 27-30°C, lows 18-20°C year-round; hill-country altitude keeps it cooler than coastal Colombo, verified as a distinct dataset rather than reused). Rainfall pattern (weather-and-climate.com / climatestotravel.com cross-check): Nov wettest (~322mm), Feb driest (~99mm), with Oct-Dec as the heaviest inter-monsoon-driven wet stretch and Jan-Mar the driest window — Kandy's rain is NOT simply the SW ("yala") monsoon like the coasts. Events: Kandy Esala Perahera 2026 dates (Aug 18-27) verified via srilankamirror.com and sridaladamaligawa.lk (official temple source); Sinhala/Tamil New Year (Apr 13-14, 2026) and Vesak Full Moon Poya (moved to May 30, 2026 per Buddhist clergy correction) verified via dailynews.lk's 2026 public holiday release.

## Track B

### Bir Billing, Himachal Pradesh (slug: `bir-billing`) — the one genuinely new city

Paragliding season: cross-checked across holidify.com, glidebirbilling.com, birbillingparagliding.com and stayvista.com — October-November is the strongest/clearest-thermal window (competitions, cross-country flying), March-May/June a longer secondary season, monsoon (~July-September) grounds flying and is rated `avoid`. Billing take-off altitude (~2,400m) confirmed via incredibleindia.gov.in and tripxl.com. Temperatures: Bir has no full Wikipedia climate infobox, so figures are drawn from climate-data.org's modelled averages (flagged as approximate, not an authoritative station record). Places verified as real: Chokling Monastery and the Bir Tibetan Colony, Deer Park Institute (founded 2006), the Bir Tea Factory cooperative, Palpung Sherabling Monastery, and Baijnath Temple (a real ASI-protected 1204 CE Shiva temple — its distance from Bir was reported inconsistently across sources, 6km vs 30km, so the entry describes it as "a half-day trip" rather than citing an unverifiable figure).

### Track B correction — 9 of the original 10 candidates were already live

The candidate list in `batch-2-candidates.md` named 10 "new city" targets (Ooty, Coorg,
Leh/Ladakh, Spiti Valley, Rameshwaram, Alleppey, McLeodganj, Mathura, Mysore, Bir), based
on a GSC query scan that checked search terms against each city's exact `name` string. That
matching missed every city whose real name carries a parenthetical or compound form —
`"Coorg (Kodagu)"`, `"Ooty (Udhagamandalam)"`, `"Spiti Valley"`, `"Leh Ladakh"`,
`"Alleppey (Alappuzha)"`, `"Mysuru (Mysore)"`, `"Mathura & Vrindavan"` — none of which match
a plain `"coorg"` / `"ooty"` substring check.

Both build agents independently caught this before writing anything (checking the existing
data model first, per the "no duplication" convention already documented at the top of
`indianCitiesExtended.ts`) and refused to create duplicate, cannibalizing pages. Verified
directly against `allCities`/`authoredMonthCitySlugs` (not the flawed script): `coorg`,
`spiti`, `ooty`, `rameswaram` (note: no "h" — different spelling than the candidate list's
`rameshwaram`), `ladakh` (covers Leh), `alleppey`, `mcleod-ganj`, `mathura`, and `mysuru`
(not `mysore`) were **already fully authored, 12/12 months, before Batch 2 started.**

One real, smaller finding survives from this: the exact slugs `mcleodganj` (no hyphen) and
`mysore` do not exist — only `mcleod-ganj` and `mysuru` do. If GSC's "[city] in [month]"
queries are specifically keyed to those exact strings, a URL redirect/alias from the missing
slug to the existing page may recover some of that impression volume — a routing decision,
not a new-content one, left for a follow-up call rather than acted on here.
