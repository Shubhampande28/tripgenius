Air India Express will launch Northeast India's first-ever direct flights to the Gulf, connecting Guwahati with both Dubai and Abu Dhabi from August 2026. It's a genuinely new corridor — until now, anyone flying from Assam or the wider Northeast to the UAE had to connect through Delhi, Mumbai or Kolkata.

## What happened

The Guwahati-Dubai service begins August 4, 2026, operating every Tuesday: departing Guwahati at 12:25 pm and landing in Dubai at 4:10 pm local time, with the return leaving Dubai at 5:10 pm and arriving back in Guwahati at 12:25 am the next day. The Guwahati-Abu Dhabi route follows on August 7, 2026, flying every Friday — departing Guwahati at 11:30 am, landing in Abu Dhabi at 3:15 pm, and returning from Abu Dhabi at 4:15 pm to reach Guwahati at 11:30 pm, per the Tribune's coverage.

This marks Air India Express' first international operations out of Guwahati, which is already one of the airline's largest stations with roughly 120 weekly domestic flights, according to Gulf News. The route is being positioned to serve tourists, students, business travellers and the Northeast's diaspora community in the Gulf, while also strengthening trade ties between the region and the UAE.

## Why it matters

Northeast India has historically had thin, expensive connectivity to international hubs outside a handful of Southeast Asian routes. A direct Gulf link cuts out a layover and a domestic connecting flight for anyone in Assam and neighbouring states heading to the UAE for work, family visits, or onward international travel via Dubai or Abu Dhabi's bigger networks.

## What this means for your trip

If you're based in Assam or nearby and have a UAE trip coming up after August 2026, compare the new direct Guwahati service against your usual Delhi or Kolkata connection — a single nonstop leg, even weekly, can save a full day of travel and a connection risk. Since it's a new route with limited weekly frequency (once a week per city), book early and build some flexibility into your dates, as new-route schedules sometimes shift in the first few months.

> Air India Express becomes "the first airline to connect Northeast India directly with West Asia," per the Tribune's June 2026 report.

## The bottom line

This is a small-frequency route today — once weekly to each city — but it's the first real Gulf gateway for Northeast India, and worth watching for added frequencies if demand holds up through 2026.
