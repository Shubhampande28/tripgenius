Etihad Airways has upgraded its India network from May 1, 2026, deploying its new Airbus A321LR — complete with enclosed First Suites and fully lie-flat Business seats — on two of its four daily flights each to Mumbai and Delhi. It's the airline's biggest premium-cabin push on Indian routes in years, alongside a wider expansion of frequencies to Bengaluru, Chennai and Hyderabad.

## What happened

The A321LR now flies two of the four daily Abu Dhabi-Mumbai and Abu Dhabi-Delhi rotations, complementing existing widebody service on Boeing 777, 787 and Airbus A350 aircraft, according to Live From A Lounge. The new jet carries two enclosed First Class suites with sliding doors, 20-inch 4K screens and fully flat beds; a 14-seat, all-window-facing lie-flat Business cabin with direct aisle access; and a 144-seat Economy cabin with 4K touchscreens and high-speed Wi-Fi.

Etihad's chairman, Mohamed Ali Al Shorafa, separately framed the move as part of a broader India expansion — Etihad now runs daily flights to Mumbai and Delhi plus additional frequencies to Bengaluru, Chennai and Hyderabad, per eplaneAI — calling it a reflection of "the enduring strength and growing promise of ties between the UAE and India."

## Why it matters

Abu Dhabi is one of the busiest connecting hubs for Indians flying onward to Europe and North America, and a consistent premium product on the Mumbai and Delhi legs — rather than a mix of older narrow-bodies and widebodies — makes connecting itineraries through AUH noticeably more comfortable for long-haul business and premium leisure travellers.

## What this means for your trip

If you're booking Etihad Business or First out of Mumbai or Delhi, check which specific flight number you're on — only two of the four daily rotations per city currently carry the A321LR's First Suites and full lie-flat Business, so it's worth timing your booking around those slots if the new cabin matters to you. If you're connecting onward in Abu Dhabi, ask your travel agent or check Etihad's booking engine for the aircraft type before you lock in dates.

## The bottom line

Etihad is betting on India as a premium growth market, not just a volume one — expect more widebody and A321LR rotations, and likely fresh route announcements to other Indian cities, as the airline leans further into the corridor through the rest of 2026.
