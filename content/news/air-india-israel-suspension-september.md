Air India has extended the suspension of its Delhi-Tel Aviv route through September 30, 2026, citing the deteriorating security situation in West Asia. The announcement, made July 22, 2026, is the airline's third extension of the suspension this year — and comes alongside a standing Indian government advisory against non-essential travel to Israel.

## What happened

Air India first suspended Tel Aviv flights earlier in 2026 amid the Iran-Israel conflict, extending the pause first to May 31, then to July 31, and now to September 30. The latest extension follows renewed US-Iran military escalation, including reported American strikes on Iranian infrastructure and Iranian retaliation against US bases in the region, according to Deccan Chronicle. A senior Air India executive was quoted saying the suspension runs "till September end due to security concerns."

Israeli carriers — El Al, IsraAir, Arkia and Air Haifa — are continuing limited operations, while most major international airlines are taking a "wait and watch" approach on the route. Separately, the Indian Embassy in Tel Aviv has repeatedly urged the roughly 40,000-plus Indian nationals in Israel to avoid non-essential travel within the country, stay near shelters, and monitor official alerts, per India's advisory covered by WION.

## Why it matters

Israel isn't a mass Indian tourist market, but it does draw religious travellers (Christian pilgrimage circuits), businesspeople, and the sizeable Indian worker and diaspora community — plus travellers who use Tel Aviv as a positioning flight. With Air India off the route until at least end-September, anyone with existing plans needs an alternative carrier or a rebooking, and anyone considering a new trip should treat the current advisory as a real deterrent, not fine print.

## What this means for your trip

If you already hold Air India tickets to Tel Aviv before September 30, expect a refund or rebooking notice — check your PNR directly rather than waiting. If you're weighing a trip to Israel in the next two months, factor in that the Indian government's own advisory currently recommends against non-essential travel, and that flight options are thinner and pricier with Air India off the route. Keep the Delhi embassy channels and the 24×7 Tel Aviv helpline (+972-54-7520711) saved if you do have people or plans there.

> "Avoid all non-essential, unnecessary travel within Israel." — Indian Embassy, Tel Aviv advisory

## The bottom line

This is now a five-month-plus suspension with no firm resumption date, and each extension has followed fresh regional escalation rather than de-escalation. Treat September 30 as a placeholder, not a promise — and check for updates before assuming Air India's Tel Aviv route resumes on schedule.
