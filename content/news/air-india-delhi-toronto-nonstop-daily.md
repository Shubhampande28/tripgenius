Air India will fly Delhi to Toronto non-stop, daily, starting August 1, 2026 — scrapping the Vienna fuel stop that has padded the route for months and cutting roughly three hours off the journey.

## What happened

The airline is upgrading the route to a Boeing 787-9 and moving it from 5-times-weekly to daily service, according to reporting by The Indian Eye. Air India had temporarily thinned out the schedule and added a Vienna technical stop earlier this year because of fuel costs and the broader West Asia conflict; the new non-stop configuration replaces that. Flight time drops from roughly 20 hours (Delhi-Toronto via Vienna) to about 17 hours non-stop, and 16 hours on the return leg.

The 787-9 deployed on the route carries 30 business class suites, 28 Premium Economy seats, and 238 Economy seats — the first time Premium Economy has been offered on this specific route.

## Why it matters

Toronto is one of Air India's most important gateways for the Indian diaspora in Canada, and the Vienna stop had made an already long journey longer and less predictable for connections. Restoring non-stop, daily service signals the airline is normalising international capacity after months of conflict-driven disruption across its long-haul network.

## What this means for your trip

- **Booking Delhi-Toronto after August 1?** Expect the non-stop 787-9 service — check your booking confirms the direct routing rather than a legacy Vienna-stop itinerary if you booked earlier.
- **Want Premium Economy on this route?** This is the first time it's been sold Delhi-Toronto — a middle option if Business is out of budget but you want more room than Economy.
- **Daily frequency means more schedule flexibility** for connecting onward within Canada or timing around work/family commitments.

> "Canada is home to one of the world's largest and most vibrant Indian diaspora communities, and Toronto has long been one of Air India's most important international gateways," said Air India CEO Campbell Wilson.

## The bottom line

After a rough stretch of fuel-driven schedule cuts across its long-haul network, Air India is putting its best aircraft back on one of its highest-demand India-Canada routes. If Toronto is on your radar for later this year, the daily non-stop from August 1 is worth checking against your existing booking.
