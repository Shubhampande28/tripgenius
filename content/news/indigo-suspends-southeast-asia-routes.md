IndiGo has temporarily suspended flights to six international destinations — Langkawi, Krabi, Ho Chi Minh City, Hong Kong, Shanghai and Siem Reap — for the July-to-September quarter, citing softer seasonal demand and a "challenging cost environment."

## What happened

The suspensions took effect July 1, 2026 for most routes and July 3 for Siem Reap, and run through September 30, according to TTR Weekly. IndiGo said bookings for all the affected routes will reopen from October 1, "subject to the geopolitical situation in the Middle East becoming favourable" — tying the pause explicitly to the ongoing West Asia conflict's effect on fuel costs and operating expenses, on top of the usual lean July-September travel quarter. The airline described the cuts as "measured changes designed to align capacity with current market conditions and demand trends," and said it would consider reinstating routes earlier with adequate notice if conditions improve. Despite the cuts, IndiGo says it still operates more than 1,800 weekly international flights.

## Why it matters

These six routes cover a mix of leisure (Langkawi, Krabi, Siem Reap), business and diaspora travel (Ho Chi Minh City) and major connecting hubs (Hong Kong, Shanghai). Losing them for three months removes IndiGo's direct options on those city pairs, pushing travellers toward other carriers, longer connections, or a rescheduled trip. It's also a concrete sign that Middle East-driven fuel costs are still reshaping Indian airlines' international networks months after the conflict began, not just through fare surcharges but through outright route pauses.

## What this means for your trip

- **Had a booking to any of these six cities on IndiGo between July and September?** Confirm your booking status directly with IndiGo — affected passengers should have been rebooked or refunded, but it's worth checking rather than assuming.
- **Planning a Langkawi, Krabi or Siem Reap trip this quarter?** Look at other direct or one-stop carriers for now; IndiGo says these routes reopen for booking October 1, so a trip in Q4 2026 or later should be unaffected.
- **Hong Kong or Shanghai for business or connections?** Budget for a routing via another carrier or hub until October, and revisit IndiGo once bookings reopen.

## The bottom line

This isn't a permanent network retreat — IndiGo has set an October 1 reopening date for bookings — but if any of these six cities were on your near-term itinerary, plan around the gap now rather than assuming your IndiGo flight will operate as booked.
